# A three-minute FINN demo

1. Open http://127.0.0.1:8000. The brief defaults to 24 hours. Show the priority disclosures, their original exchange links and the collapsed routine section. If the market window is quiet, switch to seven days; do not fabricate activity.
2. Save a filing and open the Saved tab. Show that it remains available after reloading.
3. Open Alert preferences. Change the price threshold from 3% to 1% and save. The Market signals tab recomputes against the same dated EOD prices. Restore 3% afterwards. Explain that the volume comparison excludes the latest day.
4. Open the portfolio. Show the diverse 18-company selection, session dates and price trends.
5. Open Research desk. Enter PERSISTENT or INFY, choose Promises vs. performance and investigate. The engine discovers documents for the entered symbol; it is not selecting a prewritten report.
6. Open a source passage, then its original PDF page. Explain the difference between quote verification and fully auditing a financial claim. Show the evidence-gap section if a complete comparison could not be established.
7. Try another NSE company outside the portfolio. Cached research is faster; new sources take longer. A blocked document or quota failure is disclosed and never replaced with invented company facts.

For phone layout, use a browser's responsive preview at 390 px. The default server binds to loopback; it is intentionally not exposed to your network.

Research example coverage and analysis are not guaranteed to remain identical: source indexes, reports and API availability change. Review the visible timestamps and original documents.
